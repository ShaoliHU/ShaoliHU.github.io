I am a PhD student at the [World Mind Lab](https://worldmind-lab.com/) at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST), supervised by [Prof. Fangneng Zhan](https://fnzhan.com/) and co-supervised by [Prof. Wenhan Luo](https://whluo.github.io/).

My research interests lie in **embodied AI and agentic robotics**, with a focus on how multimodal agents understand physical environments, plan actions, and coordinate robots to complete complex tasks. I am particularly interested in:

- **Multimodal Reasoning:** Connecting visual and temporal understanding with robotic decision-making.
- **Planning and Coordination:** Enabling robots to plan and collaborate on long-horizon tasks in environments with movable objects.
- **Embodied Agent Evaluation:** Designing tasks and benchmarks to assess agents’ capabilities and limitations across diverse robotic tasks and embodiments.

I’m always happy to discuss research ideas and explore potential collaborations. Feel free to get in touch! 🤗
